Delivery & Return Policy

Here's everything you need to know about your order

💳 Advance Payment

  • Regular Orders: ₹150 advance per item required to confirm your order (COD).
  • Custom T-Shirts: 50% advance required (COD).
  • Choosing Prepaid means the full order amount is paid upfront.

🚚 Delivery

  • Orders are delivered within 3–8 business days, depending on your location.
  • Please answer the delivery partner's call to ensure smooth delivery.

📦 Exchange / Wrong Product

  • Report any issue within 24 hours of delivery.
  • A 360° unboxing video is mandatory — start recording before opening the package and clearly show all sides of the parcel and the product.
  • Without a proper unboxing video, exchange or refund requests cannot be accepted.

📏 Size Policy

  • No exchange is available for size issues, since customers select their own size using our Size Guide.
  • Please check the Size Guide on the product page carefully before ordering.
